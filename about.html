Engineering in the UK. Connected to global capability.

Who we are

Engineering and project delivery, shaped by the brief.

Grandspan Engineering & Projects Limited is a UK-based engineering and project delivery company providing engineering, procurement and construction support for infrastructure and industrial projects.

From London, we coordinate multidisciplinary engineering, procurement, fabrication and construction activities around each client’s technical requirements. Our core infrastructure capabilities cover telecom towers and associated structures, alongside water supply and wastewater systems.

Our close association with Tenaro Global EPC and the Dehu Group of Companies connects Grandspan with a wider engineering and manufacturing ecosystem. This supports access to boiler and pressure parts, pressure vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, structural fabrication and customised industrial equipment.

Company status

An independent UK company, working in collaboration.

Grandspan Engineering & Projects Limited (company number 17483898) is an independent company registered in England and Wales. Its registered office is at 20 Wenlock Road, London N1 7GU.

Grandspan works in collaboration with Tenaro Global EPC Private Limited, Dehu Group companies, including Dehu Engineering (India) Private Limited, and Lero Engineering Services. These are separate legal entities. Grandspan does not own them, their factories or their facilities, and they do not own Grandspan.

  • Who does what: manufacturing, fabrication and certain engineering services described on this website are carried out by these companies. References to "the group", "the ecosystem" or "the network" mean these collaborating companies.
  • Certifications: certifications such as ASME, IBR, ISO and BS EN 1090 are held by the collaborating companies named against them, not by Grandspan.
  • Contracting: the contracting entity, scope, responsibilities and warranties for each assignment are confirmed in writing in every proposal.

Our approach

A clear route to delivery.

Each assignment moves through defined stages, so interfaces, responsibilities and approvals are visible from day one.

01

Define

Understand the site, the technical requirements, interfaces and programme, and agree the codes that apply.

02

Engineer

Concept, basic and detailed engineering, with design validation, BIM coordination and technical specifications.

03

Coordinate

Align procurement, fabrication and inspection with the agreed scope, drawings and material requirements.

04

Deliver

Construction support, testing, commissioning, as-built documentation and after-sales service.
