Cogeneration boilers

10 to 150 TPH, up to 87 kg/cm²(g)

FBC, travelling grate, dumping grate and pulsating grate designs, plus waste-heat recovery systems. They run on coal, lignite, wood, husk and other biomass, or on waste heat. Benefits include high efficiency, multi-fuel capability, an innovative bed evaporator, better steam purity, and over-bed or under-bed feed.

Heat transfer

Economisers & superheaters

Superheaters from 30 to 87 kg/cm²(g), up to 550 °C. Fin-type heat exchangers with serrated, spiral, circular or H-type fins, for waste-heat recovery boilers and hot water and hot air generators. Air preheaters.

Vessels & tanks

Pressure vessels

Feed-water heaters, coil heat exchangers, and feed-water, blowdown and flash tanks.

Auxiliaries

Plant equipment

ID and FD fans, aerofoil meters, single-flap and multi-louvre dampers, metallic and non-metallic expansion bellows, steam and gas silencers, screw conveyors and bag filters.
